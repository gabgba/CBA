Be Cineálta

What is "Be Cineálta"?

"Be Cineálta" is an Irish national anti-bullying campaign.
The word "Cineálta" comes from the Irish language and means "kind", making the campaign's message simple and powerful: Be Kind.

The campaign was developed as part of Ireland's Action Plan on Bullying and is aimed at promoting a culture of kindness,
respect, and inclusion in Irish schools and communities.


What are the goals of "Be Cineálta"?

  • To promote kindness and respect in schools across Ireland
  • To Raise awareness about the effects of bullying
  • To Encourage students, teachers, and parents to take action
  • To Build a positive school culture where everyone feels safe and included
  • To Support schools in implementing anti-bullying policies

Anti-Bullying Week

Be Cineálta is closely linked to Anti-Bullying Week in Ireland, which takes place every year in November.
During this week, schools across the country take part in events, activities,
and discussions to promote kindness and stand against bullying.


How our School gets Involved

  • By displaying Be Cineálta posters around the school
  • By running classroom discussions and workshops on bullying
